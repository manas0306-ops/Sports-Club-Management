About The Club · Heritage & Ethos

Crafted for Those Who Pursue Athletic Mastery

● Est. 1999 · New Delhi

Conceived in 1999 as an exclusive athletic sanctuary, The Champions Club bridges historical country-club elegance with cutting-edge athletic performance. Nestled on 12 manicured acres in New Delhi, our grounds were engineered with an uncompromising philosophy: that true athletic greatness requires pristine surfaces, elite sports medicine, and an inspiring sporting fraternity.

Whether you are a junior prospect training for international ITF circuits, a corporate leader preparing for competitive league ties, or an enthusiast unwinding on our glass padel courts, The Champions Club offers an environment where discipline is cultivated, rivalries are honored, and championships are forged.
