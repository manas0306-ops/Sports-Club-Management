Strict 5-Role RBAC & Authorization Traps

Primary Roles & Permitted Scopes

ADMIN /admin/dashboard (Full club analytics & audit)
MEMBER /member/dashboard (Gold, Silver, Underage passes)
PLAYER /player/dashboard (Drills, rankings & stats)
STAFF /staff/dashboard (Shifts, check-in, KOT & POS)
WALK_IN /guest (Pay-per-play pass reservations)

Server-Side Security Enforcement

  • • Zero Client Trust: The destination dashboard is strictly derived from the authenticated database account, never from client parameters.
  • • Strict HTTP 403 Trap: Cross-role access attempts (e.g. Member attempting Admin view) trigger instant HTTP 403 Forbidden and log security audit alerts.
  • • Server-Side Token Revocation: Calling /auth/logout blocks the JWT token in memory, preventing replay attacks even if the cookie persists.
  • • Junior Protection Model: Underage members (<18) enforce verified legal guardian consent and emergency phone records.

Domain Engine 1

Mathematical Court Anti-Collision Lock

Scheduling Mathematical Model

  • • 60-Minute Slots: Staggered on 30-minute intervals across all 5 courts.
  • • Overlap Condition: A collision occurs if:
    requested_start < existing_end AND requested_end > existing_start
  • • Atomic Conflict Rejection: Overlapping requests fail with HTTP 409 Conflict with timestamp details.
  • • Anti-Monopoly Quota: Strictly caps members at max 2 court sessions per day.
